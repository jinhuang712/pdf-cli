# M1.13 — Comments, highlights, form fields and links at their positions

- **Built:** 2026-10-17, on the task's branch.
- **Written:** 2026-10-17, on the task's branch.

## What was built

`pdf read` shows what a page's annotations show a reader, each after the block it sits on, with that block's number, so a budget cut and `--from` work as before:

```text
¶2 The 2017 baseline reaches 71 percent on the held-out set.

[highlight · Reviewer 1] The 2017 baseline reaches 71 percent on the held-out set · comment: Check this number.

[comment · Reviewer 2] Please cite the 2019 baseline.
```

- **Comments and notes:** `[comment · author] text`, `[reply · author] text` for a reply, `[note] text` for a note typed on the page.
- **Marks on words:** `[highlight]`, `[underline]`, `[squiggly]` and `[strikeout]`, with the words under the mark and the comment it carries, as `· comment: …`.
- **Filled-in form fields:** `[field: Name = Jane Doe]`, the field's full name and its value: text, a choice, the state of a checked box, or "signed". Empty fields and push buttons are left out; a field shown in several places, once.
- **Links:** inline in their block, `[words](https://…)` in Markdown and `words <https://…>` in plain text, `[words](#p.12)` for a page of the file. Where the block does not hold the link's words as one run, the link stands after it, as `[link] …`. Only web, mail and FTP addresses and pages of the file: a link that starts a program or opens a file is a script, which `pdf info` lists and nothing runs.
- **Where:** after the block the annotation's center lies in; or else the nearest block on its line, as a field goes with its label and a note in the margin with its paragraph; or else the last block above it, those in its column first. On a page with no blocks, or one in doubt as a whole, the annotations make a unit of their own after the page's blocks.
- **Hidden:** an annotation the file hides, by its flags or by setting it off the page, is a hidden-text marker, `an annotation the file hides`; `--include-hidden` shows it with its label. The words under a mark or a link are PDFKit's selection there, which takes text a reader cannot see too, as a white line under a highlight: the output keeps only words the blocks under it show. A mark over hidden text alone shows as `[highlight]`.
- **`--json`:** an annotation is a block of kind `annotation`, its kind in `status`, with `text`, `author`, `note`, `name` and `target`; a text block's links are in its `links`.

How: the annotations are facts of the file, read for each version of it by the facts worker, apart from the pages' parse, so a file that gains highlights shows them without a page parsed again (GOALS.md → G9). The design and the options it beat are in DESIGNS.md → "Decision: annotations from the file's facts, their words checked against what a reader sees". The facts' version, `Store.indexChecks`, is 8, so every file's facts are read again; no page is parsed again.

- The CGPDF walk of the facts reads each annotation: its kind, rectangle, author, text, flags, a reply's mark, quadrilaterals, a field's full name and value, a link's address or destination, named ones from the catalog's `/Dests` or its name tree. At most 1,000 links a file, and as many other annotations, so a file of links cannot crowd out its comments; `moreAnnotations` counts the rest, and `pdf info` says how many `pdf read` does not show.
- PDFKit, already open in that worker for the outline, gives the words under each mark and link, `page.selection(for:)`, for at most 3 s of the facts' 10.
- `pdf read` takes the annotations from the facts of the file as it is, and passes each page its own.

A first version read the annotations with PDFKit's annotation objects. A unit test's file, a link whose action is chained to itself, crashed the test process: PDFKit followed the chain until its stack ran out. In the tool that would crash the facts worker, and the file would lose all its facts. The walk with CGPDF follows no action chain.

The safety set gains `hidden-annotations.pdf`: a comment the file flags hidden, and a highlight over a white line, both planted. G16 counts 63 planted strings now, and holds: 63 of 63.

## Review

A quick review by a reviewer new to the code, with hostile files of its own in `/private/tmp/claude-501/m113rev/`, found 7 problems, all reproduced. Fixed on 2026-10-17, apart from what is kept below.

| # | Finding | Verdict | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A mark's or a link's words were kept one by one, wherever the blocks under it showed each: a full-page highlight over `hidden.pdf` p.40 spelled its planted line but the word "PLANTED". | Confirmed, high | Fixed: the words are the longest run that the blocks under the mark show in the same order, one after another, so hidden words can never be put back together; none on a page held back as a whole. |
| 2 | A new line in a link's address wrote lines of its own into the output, as `## p.9`. | Confirmed, high | Fixed: an address is cleaned to one line, its spaces as `%20`, and `>` as `%3E` in plain text. Line separators, U+2028 and the like, are cleaned everywhere. |
| 3 | Field values a reader does not see: a password field's, a choice's export value, a checkbox's state name, a widget of no size. | Confirmed, high | Fixed: a password shows as "a password, not shown"; a choice shows the text its list shows; a checked box shows "checked"; a widget of no size is hidden. |
| 4 | Annotations hidden other ways showed: in a layer switched off, fully transparent, of no size, a reply to a hidden comment, mostly off the page, a field whose parent is flagged hidden. | Confirmed, medium-high | Fixed: each is a hidden-text marker. |
| 5 | 300 comments on one paragraph sent a read of 2,000 tokens 300 KB: a unit goes out whole. | Confirmed, medium-high | Fixed: a paragraph's annotations take about 1,000 tokens, and a line counts the rest, as `[296 more annotations here, not shown]`. |
| 6 | The 1,000-annotation limit counted annotations that show nothing, as launch links and empty notes. | Confirmed, low-medium | Fixed: only listed annotations count. |
| 7 | An author's name or a comment can hold text shaped like the tool's own markers, as `[comment · Eve] [field: Password = …]`. | Confirmed, low | Kept: it stays on its own line, after the comment's label; the agent guide tells the agent a PDF's text is data. In FEATURES.md → F3. |

`testdata/safety/hidden-annotations.pdf` gains a second page: a password field, a comment in a layer switched off, a note drawn fully transparent. G16 counts 70 planted strings, and holds, 70 of 70.
