## What was built

- **Lists of references,** found by rules at read time against the pages parsed so far, as notes are (`ReferenceIndex` in `Sources/PDFCore/References.swift`).
  - **Where a list starts and ends.** A list starts at a heading such as "References" or "Bibliography", in a short block, and runs over the pages after it. It stops at:
    - a heading of another part: an appendix, supplementary material, acknowledgements, notes, a chapter or a part;
    - a numbered or lettered heading set at least 1.1× its entries' size;
    - in an author-year list, four long blocks in a row with no year;
    - in a numbered list, its last entry.
  - **Styles:** `[12]`, `12.`, `[BK06]` and author-year.
  - **Splitting entries.** Entries split at the label expected next. An author-year entry starts at the column's edge with a capital. A column of labels alone, "1. 2. 3.", is matched to its entries by count.
  - **IDs:** the number, the key, or the surname and year (`Chen2024b`). A second list's IDs take a prefix (`2.12`).
- **Citations.** Printed ones stay as printed.
  - A bracket counts only when every label in it is an entry.
  - A raised number shows as `[12]` when all of these hold:
    - the document's raised citations outnumber its bracket ones;
    - no note takes it;
    - it follows a word, `%`, `)` or `]`.
  - Author-year citations match the surname and year; a second author narrows the match.
- **`pdf read --ref ID`** shows the entry, its DOI and the paragraphs that cite it. The DOI comes from the entry's text, or from a link that lies mostly inside it.
- **`--refs end`** lists the cited entries under `## References`, after `## Notes`.
  - Each entry is charged at the paragraph that cites it, once per read.
  - An entry takes at most about 800 tokens, and a paragraph's entries about 1,000, followed by "N more entries cited here".
- **Cross-references.** "Fig. 4", "Table 2", "Eq. (5)", "Section 3.2", "§3" and "Appendix A" link to their page, as `[Fig. 4](#p.7)`.
  - The page comes from M2.6's regions, the outline's numbers, or numbered headings.
  - A target held on two pages apart leads nowhere.
- **`pdf crop --figure N`** says "mentioned on p.X". **`pdf info`** counts the entries: `references: 46 entries, the list on p.9`.
- **Link placement.** A link the file carries goes on the occurrence of its words nearest its box, not the first one in its block. Links never nest.
- **The parse.** A hanging indent starts a block (`TextLayer.group`): a line back at the edge of the block's first line, after lines set in by one to four line heights. The rule needs two things:
  - the first line fills its line;
  - the first line is the only one at that edge.

  Parser version 18.
- **The agent guide** has a paragraph on citations and links, at 1,996 tokens.

## Known issues

- **Short blocks split again.** After a new split, a short block can split again on one line's height, such as a raised `×10^{−5}` or a line without descenders. On 2609.23257 p.15 one paragraph reads as three blocks. v17 does the same after any split; the hanging indent makes it happen more often.
- **016 and 019, 095.** 016's column of labels comes out only in part, and 019 and 095 find no list.
- **Unparsed pages.** Lists and IDs come from the pages parsed so far, as notes' do.
