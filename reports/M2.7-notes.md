## What was built

Notes are found by rules, with no model, from a font size kept per line (`TextLine.size`), markers and positions, in the parse (parser version 17). What depends on the whole document, which pages are a list of endnotes, each note's reference and the numbering, is judged when a page is read, against the pages parsed so far, as M2.5's running headers are (`Notes.resolve`, `NoteIndex`).

- A **footnote** is a small block in the lower 60% of the page that starts with its marker, a raised number or sign, or a number the text raises, with no body-size text below it in its column. The parse breaks a block where a line starts with a raised marker, or the next number in a run of small lines, so a column of notes set with no gap comes apart.
- An **endnote** is an entry of a run of three or more rising numbers under a heading "Notes", "Endnotes" or "Notes to Chapter 3", running headers included, and on the pages that go on from it. Entries set as running text, as the 9/11 Commission Report's, come apart where the next number follows.
- A **headnote** is a small paragraph under a chapter's first heading; a **margin note** is a block of running words wholly beside the page's columns; an **author note** is a footnote of the first two pages under a sign or about the authors.

**`pdf read`** puts `[^7]` where a note is cited and its words after the output, under `## Notes`, as `[^7] (p.3) …`. `--notes inline` puts each by the paragraph of its page that cites it, `--notes off` leaves them out and counts them in the summary line. A margin note follows the paragraph it stands beside, `[margin]`; a headnote stays in place, `[headnote]`. Numbering that restarts makes a series, and the ID carries it, as `3.7`; the first series stays plain, so IDs do not change while later pages parse. `pdf read paper.pdf --footnote 7` parses the whole file and shows one note whole with the places that cite it. **`pdf info`** counts the notes and lists the author notes.

Bounds, each counted where it cuts: a page's notes take about 4,000 tokens and one note 800 (`--footnote` shows any whole); a paragraph's margin notes about 1,000, the rest reading in place; the budget stops a read at a note's block as at a paragraph's.
