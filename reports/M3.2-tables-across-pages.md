## What was built

A table that runs over page breaks is found as one table region per page. `pdf read` now links the regions, `pdf info` marks them, and `pdf crop --table N` gives one crop per part. The tool converts nothing: it says which crops belong together and the agent reads them (plan/decisions/059-a-table-s-parts-are-linked-at-read-time-only-on-a-continued.md → "Decision: a table's parts are linked at read time, only on a continued caption and the same place on both pages").

- **Judged at read time** in `Sources/PDFCore/TableLinks.swift`, from the stored regions, against the pages parsed so far. No parse change (parser version stays 21), no cache table, so there is no old-cache case.
- **Two regions on neighbouring pages are linked** only when: same number; the later caption, as a reader sees it (hidden text counts for nothing), says it goes on or repeats the earlier one in full (16 letters or more); the earlier part ends in the page's bottom quarter with no figure or table below it in its columns, the later starts in the top quarter with none above; same left edge and width within 4%; each is the other's only candidate.
- **Output.** `read`: `continues on p.N` / `continued from p.N` and `linked parts: …` under the block, a `table` object in `--json` (`id`, `part`, `parts`, `pages`, `continuesOn`, `continuedFrom`, `openBefore`, `openAfter`). `info`: the rows and `--json`. `crop --table N`: every linked part, one image each, `parts` in `--json`.
- **A read parses what the chain needs**: the page past a table that ends its page, the page before one whose caption says it goes on, one page at a time, up to 60.
- Fixtures: `testdata/tables` (continued, separate, narrow, repeated, hidden-continued, control, gap), made by `scripts/make-hidden-fixture.swift`.

## Findings of the review and their resolution

1. **High: the "continued" words matched other captions** ("(control vs. treatment)", "(contrast…)", "(continuous…)", "(context length)", "[suite A]"). Fixed: the keyword must end at a non-letter and may be followed only by from/on/of/von/de/di… and what they name; the Chinese and Japanese forms are their own branch; the end-of-caption form needs a dash, colon or period, or the label straight before it. the bad captions and the good ones are unit tests; `control.pdf` links on 88725ef and not now.
2. **Medium: chain gaps were silent.** Fixed: `may go on past p.N, not linked` and `continued from an earlier page, not linked`, and `openAfter`/`openBefore` in `--json`. A lone table that ends low says nothing. FEATURES' "12 pages" is 13 and is corrected. `gap.pdf` tests it.
3. **Medium: `crop --table` judged ambiguity before the walk parsed more pages.** Fixed: the regions are found again after the walk (`gap.pdf`: read p.1, then `crop --table 1` now fails with "add --page"; with `--page 1` it crops the two linked parts).
4. **Medium: a kept reading belonged to one page.** Fixed: the reading of a linked table is looked up on every page of the chain, shown on the first part, and the other parts say where; `crop` shows it too.
5. **Medium: mirrored margins give no links** (textbook 070). Not fixed, by decision: FEATURES.md Gap and plan/TODO.md.
6. **Low.** The crop-all note moved off the crop command's line; DESIGNS no longer says "one extra page". The guard of no body text between parts (two studies in one file, "study2") is not cheap: FEATURES Gap and TODO. Table numbers such as S.6.3.a and S.6.3.b merge in the parser; only the caption test keeps them apart: FEATURES Gap. The agent guide is at 1,999 of 2,000 tokens.
