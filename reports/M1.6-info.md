# M1.6 — `pdf info`, and reads by section and printed page

- **Built:** 2026-10-15 in `b8f2d70`, with review fixes in the commit after it.
- **Written:** 2026-10-15, on the task's branch, after the review fixes.

## What was built

- The index reads each file's facts: author and year, bookmarks with their spot on the page, page labels, comments, highlights, form fields and whether they are filled, attached files, and scripts and launches with what would start them. They are kept with the file's entry (schema 6, index checks 7 after the fixes).
- `pdf info` gives the title, authors, year, DOI, arXiv ID, author notes, page count, columns, pages from the text layer and from OCR, tokens, sections with pages and sizes, the counts, attachments, scripts and flagged pages. It is provisional until every page is parsed, and `--json` gives everything.
- Sections come from bookmarks, placed on the block that spells their title, else from headings in the text.
- `pdf read --section` reads from a heading to the next section at its level or above; `--printed-page` reads by page label.

## Found while fixing

- **My own regressions, caught by comparing all 11 samples before and after:** the first fix missed numbers such as "A.1" before a heading, which moved five appendix sections onto the wrong block; and passing over a next section that sits above made "5.4 Qualitative Analysis" swallow the rest of the file. Both are fixed: the samples now differ from before only in the two corrections listed in finding 8 and below.
- **"5.4 Qualitative Analysis"** on arXiv 2608.26848 comes after "6. Conclusion" in the text's reading order. It measured 0 tokens before the fixes; it now covers the rest of its page, 573 tokens.
- **The index takes 30 s on the 30,000-page file** (3.9 s on 10,000), because it asks CGPDF for each page by number. That code predates M1.6, so it is logged in TODO, not fixed here.
