# M2.19: hidden text through OCR

- **Landed:** 2026-10-25, built in `81d2af8`, with the review's fixes after it.
- **Parser version 20** (was 19); page key 2, index checks 10 and cache schema 8 are unchanged.

## What was built

M2.10's review (H1) left one hole: text PDFKit leaves out of its text, which the page still draws, could be read by OCR as plain text. The check found it real.

- OCR reads PDFKit's rendering, which draws a form in a layer switched off and leaves annotations out. A Type 3 font with no text map, whose glyphs are real letters, in such a form or under an annotation, was read as plain text on a scan's first reading and after `pdf reparse --step ocr`, and `pdf search` found it.
- `Visibility.unseen()` finds such strings from the page's drawing commands. A string counts where the page drawn without annotations (`Visibility.bare`, kept only when the page has annotations to draw) has ink in its tight glyph box, and either its form is switched off or the page as a reader sees it has no ink there. Touching places on a line merge; up to 500 are kept.
- `TextLayer.read` gives the places to the hidden spots that `OCR.markHidden` already uses (reason: leaves no ink). On more than 500 places, or where the drawing check cannot reach, the page gets `hiddenCheckIncomplete`; the worker now copies that flag from the text layer onto an OCR result in both OCR branches.
- Fixtures: `testdata/hidden/unread-hidden.pdf` (4 pages) and `unread-hidden-more.pdf` (11 pages), made by `scripts/make-hidden-fixture.swift`, same bytes each run. They are not in `planted.tsv`: the plain read has no marker, as PDFKit's text does not hold the words, so the manifest check would fail; `scripts/safety-check.sh` has its own OCR checks for both.

## Measurements

- On the build before the fix (`81d2af8`, built in a scratch worktree), `unread-hidden-more.pdf` after `--step ocr` leaked `PLANTED` on pages 2, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9 (5 search hits), hid the visible words on page 11, and timed out on page 5. After the fix, none of those leaks; pages 6 says its check is incomplete; the controls (pages 10, 11) read as text.
- `hidden.pdf` (73 pages), `scan-naca-report-1034.pdf` and `unread.pdf` read after `--step ocr`, new against old: the same text; the only differences were "time limit" failures that moved between pages on both builds under load, and the new incomplete note on `hidden.pdf` page 23.
- Debug build: the second fixture reparsed in about 2 s.
