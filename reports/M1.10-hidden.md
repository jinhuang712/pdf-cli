# M1.10 — Hidden text

- **Built:** 2026-10-15 in `7378826`, with review fixes in the commit after it.
- **Written:** 2026-10-15, on the task's branch.

## What was built

- `testdata/hidden/hidden.pdf`, written by hand by `scripts/make-hidden-fixture.swift`: one page per trick, each with visible lines and a planted line, plus two pages whose text a reader can see in an unusual way.
- `Visibility`: each page drawn in grey at 2 px a point, and each word checked for ink in its selection box, for type under 2 pt, and against images drawn over text after it. `Drawing` reads the page's drawing commands for covering images, text placed off the page, and a scan's image under its text layer, strips included.
- Hidden words are marked while lines are built and stored as `Hidden` runs on each block; text off the page is stored on the page. Parser version 6.
- `pdf read` shows a marker in place of each run, with its size, reason and the command that shows it; `--include-hidden` shows the words, labelled; the summary line and the JSON count the places by page.
- Hidden words stay out of the keyword index, of `--regex` search, and of the title, DOI and sections `pdf info` finds.

Found while building, and fixed before the review:

- PDFKit leaves text placed off the page out of the page's text entirely, and widening the page's bounds does not bring it back, so the drawing commands spell it.
- The 1951 scan draws each page as 33 image strips, and before its text on some pages and after it on others. A single-image, image-first rule marked 21 of its old OCR layer's stray marks as hidden; the rule now adds the strips up, in either order.
- PDFKit's per-character boxes sat two characters off on a line that changes color mid-way, so a hidden word was tested against its visible neighbour's ink. Word boxes now come from PDFKit's selection.
- A slanted chart label, "resection margins", read as having no ink: its boxes miss its ink. Words set at an angle are no longer judged by ink.
- Single maths symbols whose boxes PDFKit places off their ink read as hidden; a run needs 3 letters or digits.
- The Swift compiler crashed on my first scanner, whose C callbacks captured closures; each operator now has a plain callback.

## Review

The reviewer wrote 39 pages of its own, hostile and harmless, and ran every sample. It found real problems both ways: text a reader sees marked hidden, the worst failure, and planted text let through. The fixes changed the design, which DESIGNS records; the samples still show only the one true marker.

| # | Finding | Verdict | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | An image drawn after text counted as covering it with no regard to its clip, transparency or blend: text beside a cropped picture and under a faint watermark read as hidden. | Confirmed, high | Fixed: OCR reads the area of any image drawn after text, and only a word OCR does not find is covered. Soft-masked images, which were skipped, are now checked the same way. |
| 2 | Scan strips drawn after their text layer marked the scan's words covered. | Confirmed, high | Fixed: the scan test runs after the whole page is read, and a scan's page skips the covering rule. |
| 3 | Colored text whose grey matched its background, as red on mid grey, read as hidden: the ink test was grey. | Confirmed, high | Fixed: the page is drawn in color, and a pixel is ink by hue or by contrast. |
| 4 | Any page whose images added up to 80%, overlaps counted twice, switched off every check but covering: white, invisible and tiny text on a slide's background passed as text. | Confirmed, high | Fixed in part: images are counted on a grid, so overlaps count once, and a scan's page keeps the color and size checks. Text in the invisible mode over a page-size image still reads as text, as a scan's layer must; recorded in FEATURES. |
| 5 | An image with an opaque soft mask covered text unflagged. | Confirmed, high | Fixed, by OCR as in 1. |
| 6 | Words with one visible letter each, the rest in the color behind them, read in full. | Confirmed, high | Fixed: a word with letters of different colors is checked letter by letter. |
| 7 | Text set at an angle skipped the ink test, an easy way through. | Confirmed | Not fixed: PDFKit's boxes for angled text miss its ink, so even "no ink at all" misfired on a slanted chart label. Recorded in FEATURES. |
| 8 | Smaller ways through: type squeezed to 1%, glyphs stacked by negative spacing, a cover 5 forms deep, and 200,001 empty strings that ran out the command limit. | Confirmed | Fixed: squeezed and stacked letters count as too small to read; forms are read 8 deep; empty strings do not count; a page past a limit says so in place. |
| 9 | A form in a layer switched off, which CoreGraphics draws. | Confirmed | Fixed: the words such a form shows are hidden where it shows them. |
| 10 | `--raw` showed hidden words with no marker, in doubtful text and in a scan's replaced layer. | Likely | Fixed: doubtful text gets markers, and the replaced layer keeps only visible text. |
| 11 | When the budget cut a block holding a marker, its count went to the next read. | Confirmed | Fixed. |
| 12 | An unbalanced `q` inside a form broke the scanner's transform stack, and real text read as off the page. | Confirmed | Fixed: a form's stack ends with it. PDFKit leaves that text out of the page's text, so it goes missing again; logged in TODO. |

## Second review

The same reviewer re-checked `15bda2e` on its earlier pages, two new files of its own and every sample. Most of round one holds: the false alarms on cropped pictures, watermarks, scan strips and colored text are gone, the samples still give only "vascular lesion", and a text page costs about 48 ms at the median in its measurement. It found these, **all open** when this was written; M1.10 lands only once they are fixed or recorded as limits.

| # | Finding | Verdict | Status and suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | A page with a page-size background image keeps only the color and size checks, so black text under an opaque image, clipped text, text under a white box and squeezed text all read as text there. One white 1×1 image stretched over the page is enough. | Confirmed, high | Open. Exempt only text in the invisible modes, 3 and 7, which the drawing scanner can track, and keep the ink, covering and squeeze rules for text drawn in fill mode. |
| B | The color behind a word, taken from the box's border, is fooled by two grey rules drawn along the border: white text between them reads as ink. Table rules or a cell's frame do the same. | Confirmed, high | Open. Take the background from the box's inside too, the box's most common color when it disagrees with the border's, or count only ink inside the glyphs' outlines. |
| C | One visible letter a word still works when the rest is hidden by render mode, a clip or a white shape, not by color: letters are checked one by one only when their colors differ. | Confirmed, medium | Open. Check letter by letter whenever a word's ink is low for its letter count, or track the render mode per character. |
| D | Fragments leak: 2-letter pieces of words hidden letter by letter, "to one" from squeezed text, "by", "one" and "on" from stacked glyphs. | Confirmed, low | Open; harmless alone. |
| E | The stack rule marks real words of narrow letters in condensed type: "lilt fill tilt" at 85% horizontal scale read as too small to read. It measures each box's longer side, its height, against the run's length. | Confirmed, high | Open. Compare the run's width with the sum of the boxes' widths, or with the font size times the horizontal scale. |
| F | Text in a script Apple OCR cannot read, as Greek in the Symbol font, under a transparent image read as covered. Hebrew, Devanagari and many maths fonts are at the same risk. | Confirmed, high | Open. Call a word covered only when the image changed the pixels over it, by drawing the box with and without the image or reading the soft mask's alpha, and use OCR only for opaque images. |
| G | No cap on OCR calls a page: 200 labels under 200 transparent images took 4.3 s for one page. | Confirmed, medium | Open. Merge the covering areas before OCR, cap the calls a page, and mark the page's check incomplete past the cap. |

The reviewer could not rerun `scripts/test.sh` at the end, because the session had moved to another worktree; its last check found no process left running. Its files are in `/private/tmp/claude-501/m110rev/`, made by `gen.py` and `gen2.py`, outside the repo.

Found while fixing: the first OCR check read only an icon's own box and missed the label half under it, and OCR reads a comic font's "XSports" as "sports"; the area now has a margin, and one missed letter is allowed. The color test at first ran a second full count for every visible word, which took a text page to 84 ms; it now reads the color behind a word from the box's border, and counts ink until there is enough.
