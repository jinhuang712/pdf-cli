- **Built:** 2026-10-17, on the task's branch.

## What was built

`pdf read` marks each image and drawing a page shows, after the block it sits on, with the command that crops it:

```text
¶2 Visible words after the trick on this page.

[image] crop: pdf crop testdata/safety/carries-pictures.pdf --page 1 --bbox 0.11,0.30,0.34,0.14

[drawing] crop: pdf crop testdata/safety/carries-pictures.pdf --page 1 --bbox 0.52,0.30,0.37,0.14
```

- **Images:** each the page shows, filling its bounds at half opacity or more, 0.5% of the page or more, clipped to the page. None on a scan's page, whose image is the page.
- **Drawings:** the hidden-text check's pass over the drawing commands now records each shape painted to show: a path with a curve or a slanted line, a filled shape wider and taller than a rule, a bar, or a rule, a line or a thin rectangle along the page's axes. A rectangle with rounded corners, a few small curves between straight sides, counts as a rectangle. Shapes within 6 pt of one another join into a region; a region is a drawing when it holds a curve or a slanted line, or two bars, and covers 1% of the page or more. None of these makes a drawing:
  - rules alone, such as a table's borders or an underline;
  - a box with rounded corners around text;
  - a bar with text on it, such as a table's shaded cell;
  - a bar at the page's edge, such as a band down the margin;
  - a shape that fills most of the page, such as a background.
- A figure's panels side by side, 30 pt apart at most, join into one. A drawing's crop takes in the text within 12 pt of it, its labels, title and legend.
- **Stamps, ink and shapes** a reader drew on the page are annotations from the file's facts, as M1.13's: `[stamp] Approved · crop: …`, `[ink]`, `[shape]`. A stamp with no text of its own shows its name only when the name is one of the 14 the PDF standard defines, as "Approved" or "Not Approved". `Store.indexChecks` 9, so a file indexed before gains them on its next read.
- At most 30 pictures a page, the largest. They count against the 1,000 tokens a paragraph's annotations may take. Those on one row, starting within a hundredth of the page of each other, go left to right.
- A page read by OCR keeps the pictures its drawing commands show.
- The regions are part of the page's parse, `PageText.pictures`: parser version 13.

## Limits

- A figure is not yet a figure: no caption, no alt text, and a figure's panels stacked or far apart are a marker each. M2.6 detects figures, tables and equations.
- Two figures side by side in two columns, with a gutter under 30 pt, join into one marker: the review's two charts with a caption each. The same rule joins one figure's panels, which matters more.
- Images in a form clipped to a path that is no rectangle, and images drawn with a mask, may count as shapes or not at all: a photo clipped to a rounded path in OpenStax's p.113 is a `[drawing]`.
- A heatmap drawn as cells with a number on each has no marker: each cell is a bar with text on it.
- The joining of regions takes passes × n² comparisons of at most 2,000 shapes: a page built to need one join per pass, 80 strokes in a spiral, took 6.4 s to parse in the debug build, under the 30 s a page may take. In `TODO.md`.
- A marker goes after the block it sits on, which inside a chart can be one of its tick labels.
