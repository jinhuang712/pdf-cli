## What was built

`pdf read` marks each image and drawing a page shows, after the block it sits on, with the command that crops it:

```text
¶2 Visible words after the trick on this page.

[drawing] crop: pdf crop testdata/safety/carries-pictures.pdf --page 1 --bbox 0.52,0.30,0.37,0.14

[image] crop: pdf crop testdata/safety/carries-pictures.pdf --page 1 --bbox 0.11,0.30,0.34,0.14
```

- **Images:** each the page shows, filling its bounds at half opacity or more, 0.5% of the page or more. None on a scan's page, whose image is the page.
- **Drawings:** the hidden-text check's pass over the drawing commands now records each shape painted to show: a path with a curve or a slanted line, a filled rectangle wider and taller than a rule, or a rule. Shapes within 6 pt of one another join into a region; a region is a drawing when it holds a curve or a slanted line, or two bars, and covers 1% of the page or more. Rules alone, as a table's borders or an underline, are none; nor is a bar with text on it, as a table's shaded cell, nor a shape that fills most of the page, as a background. A figure's panels side by side, 30 pt apart at most, join into one.
- **Stamps, ink and shapes** a reader drew on the page are annotations from the file's facts, as M1.13's: `[stamp] Approved · crop: …`, `[ink]`, `[shape]`, with the stamp's name where it says nothing else. `Store.indexChecks` 9, so a file indexed before gains them on its next read.
- At most 30 pictures a page, the largest. They count against the 1,000 tokens a paragraph's annotations may take.
- The regions are part of the page's parse, `PageText.pictures`: parser version 13.

## Measurements

On the smoke set, release build: the NACA scan has no marker; the ten papers have 1 to 19 images and 0 to 10 drawings each. Spot checks by crop: a bar chart drawn as shapes is a drawing, a chart embedded as an image an image, an algorithm box drawn with three rules nothing. Figure 1 of the dataset-distillation paper, three panels with photos and diagrams, was five drawings before the panels side by side joined, two after.

## Limits

- A figure is not yet a figure: no caption, no alt text, and a figure's panels stacked or far apart are a marker each. M2.6 detects figures, tables and equations.
- Images in a form clipped to a path that is no rectangle, and images drawn with a mask, may count as shapes or not at all.
