# M1.7 — `pdf read`

- **Landed:** 2026-10-15 in `22ce382`, with review fixes in `173dc43` and the user's label choice in `5185aa0`.
- **Written:** after the fact, on 2026-10-15.

## What was built

- `pdf read` prints a PDF page by page from `--page` specs or the first page, within a 5,000-token budget. A `next:` line goes on with `--from p4.27` or `--from p3.2:200`.
- `--json` gives every block its page and box; `--format md|text`; `--raw`.
- The summary says provisional while pages are still parsing. Pages are fetched a few at a time, so an early stop waits only for its own pages.
- Raised and lowered characters smaller than the line's main text get `^{…}` and `_{…}`, from PDFKit's baseline offsets.
- Each character's glyph box is matched to its selection, because PDFKit's `characterBounds` shifts by one after every ligature.
- Page labels show in page headings. Parser version 5.
- **The user's choice, `5185aa0`:** text output starts each page with `## p.N` and each block with `¶N`; positions stay in `--json`.

## Review

The reviewer built the commit, ran the 97 unit tests, and wrote the chaining script above.

| # | Finding | Verdict | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A unit larger than the budget that did not start with a text block made `next:` repeat the same command forever. | Confirmed | Fixed: the first unit after a page's heading always goes out, cut inside when it is text. |
| 2 | False `_{…}` marks on scans with an old OCR layer, as in "title _{50,} _{sec.} _{151).}" on the 1951 scan. This was wrong text. | Confirmed | Fixed: a whole word standing alone keeps no mark, and the lowering threshold rose to 8%. |
| 3 | After a glyph shift of more than 2, the matching never re-aligned, and the rest of the page fell back to selection boxes: 1,720 characters on one page. | Confirmed | Fixed: boxes are checked line by line against the line's selection, with per-character matching only on lines a ligature shifts, and a wider search after a jump. |
| 4 | A page whose heading alone went out counted as read, with all its flags and counts. | Confirmed by trace | Fixed: counts come only from units shown. |
| 5 | An unsure break inside a marked run dropped the block's later unsure spans. | Confirmed by trace, rare | Fixed: unsure words compare with marks left out. |
| 6 | Text that really starts with "…" repeated one character after a cut. | Confirmed by trace, minor | Fixed. |
| 7 | A later chunk that failed, or a file that changed mid-read, ended the read silently. | Likely | Fixed: the read stops with a warning and a `next:` line. |
| 8 | A huge `--page` range allocated the whole list before the range check. | Confirmed by reading | Fixed: ranges past 100,000 pages are refused. |
| 9 | DESIGNS and FEATURES described the cut loosely, `--full` was unexplained, and tests missed doubts over the budget and repeated cuts. | Docs and tests | Fixed: the docs, and new tests for a chained read, a doubt larger than the budget, a heading-only page, and a whole small word. |
