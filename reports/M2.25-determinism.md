## What was built

The task chased everything that made the same file read differently, or failed on `main` since PDFKit and Vision changed on this machine on 2026-10-25. Four causes, one of them ours.

1. **The space that flipped on page 17 of `arxiv-2609.23257.pdf` ("Output (JSON)" against "Output(JSON)").** Not PDFKit and not our code: PDFKit's text of the page was the same in 130 runs (30 separate processes and 100 loops of one), and the rendered image hashed the same each time. Apple's document recognizer returned two different texts for the same image in different processes, never twice in one process (40 calls in a loop, 8 in parallel tasks, also in the background state). The first investigation saw it in 8 of 12 full reads; the review saw it in 2 of 12; later series (12, 14 and 16 fresh processes, also under load) showed none. The space repair now reads with the text recognizer (`VNRecognizeTextRequest`, accurate), which gave one answer for every page in 8 runs of 8 and 12 of 12. Changed: `SpaceRepair.swift`, `Environment.swift` (parser version 21). Smoke flags of `arxiv-2609.23257` changed: the text recognizer finds "CTRL performs" where the document recognizer left unsure spans (p.2, 9, 12 gone) and leaves a real unsure span on p.17. plan/decisions/056-the-space-repair-reads-with-the-text-recognizer.md → "Decision: the space repair reads with the text recognizer".
2. **Visible prose that OCR hid as "too small to read" on `sottigra.pdf` (TeX Live's MusiXTeX manual), pages 3, 8 and 10. Ours.** The spot is the union of the boxes of a music font's squeezed glyphs; one of them, a staff rule, is 516 points wide in PDFKit's boxes (from x = -51), so p.8's spot is 95% of the page wide and 14% tall, and `OCR.markHidden` hid every line inside it. Text that small draws as specks, and OCR reads specks as stray marks, so a line that reads as prose (three words of two letters or more, letters in 60% of its marks) is no longer covered by a `tiny` spot; other reasons still hide every line they cover. On `sottigra.pdf` the prose is text and only "3 3g", a row of Arabic digits, "B.... b...." and "cresc." stay under the marker. All 73 pages of `testdata/hidden/hidden.pdf`, read by OCR, keep every planted word out. New fixture `testdata/hidden/tiny-near-prose.pdf` (`scripts/make-hidden-fixture.swift`, the other fixtures' bytes unchanged), `TinySpotsTests`. plan/decisions/057-an-ocr-line-that-reads-as-prose-is-no-too-small-to-read-text.md → "Decision: an OCR line that reads as prose is no 'too small to read' text".
3. **The safety check's `hidden/hidden.pdf` p.38: a real leak of "user." (macOS 27's PDFKit).** The text of the page is "...the user.REDACTEDREDACTED...": the labels start back at the left, no space is put between the runs, and the boxes for the characters at the join are 140 and 200 points wide, so the joined word took ink from the labels and read as seen. First fix: judge a word as two, split before the first character whose box is over three times its font size (superseded, see H2 and M1). Final fix: a word whose letters are checked one by one now includes punctuation between letters, such as the "." of "user.REDACTED". New safety file `testdata/safety/hidden-joins.pdf` (7 pages, listed in `planted.tsv`; the review's version fails on the base build).
4. **Tests and checks that failed on `main` for reasons outside our code.** (a) `readShowsRegionsInPlaceOfTheirText`: PDFKit on macOS 27 puts U+FFFC in its text for a tagged figure, one per picture; it showed as a block "[figure text] ￼" and as a fifth set-aside block (four expected), and the smoke papers `arxiv-2608.23474` and `arxiv-2608.26848` printed lines "¶2 ￼ ￼ ￼ ￼" in normal output. The first fix counted U+FFFC as a space (changed in the review, H1). (b) `aPDFKitSaveChangesTheTextSoTheKeysChange`: all 11 pages of the sample paper read the same after a PDFKit save on this macOS, so the test keeps what is still true, that the page keys change, and is renamed (`PageKeyTests`); the saves decision stays, as older saves keep their damage (a dated note was added to it). (c) `scripts/test-cache.sh` "--show raw shows its text, labelled": the document recognizer reads the sideways stamp "TECH LIBRARY KAFB, NM" as "LIBRARY" and "KAFB, NM" with "TECH" gone (3 of 3 runs), the text recognizer still reads all four words; the check now expects the title "NATIONAL ADVISORY COMMITTEE", which both read.
5. **Tests that took page 2's unsure paragraph of `arxiv-2609.23257` as their fixture** (the pick, dry-run and reparse tests and `test-cache.sh`) now use page 3, paragraph 5 ("lookback L and F features"), the first unsure paragraph, since "CTRLperforms" now breaks cleanly. The reparse test with a planted crash crashes page 3 (`crash:2`) and reparses 3,4; the chunk test reparses 3-10. `test-cache.sh`: `hidden.pdf` p.38 also reports the last letter of the last REDACTED label, 3 points past the black box (58 places in the first commit, 59 after the review).
