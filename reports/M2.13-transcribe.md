## Why

The user, on 2026-10-18: the tool converts nothing it cannot read for certain. Rules or OCR cannot promise an equation's LaTeX or a table's cells right, and a wrong one looks right, so the agent's own model reads them from their crops. What the agent reads must not be lost: "看完也需要产出 latex 的文本，否则无法后续基于cache重读", it must produce the LaTeX, or later reads cannot come from the cache. This replaced M2.13's first plan, LaTeX rebuilt by rules from the glyphs (DESIGNS.md → "Decision: the agent reads figures, tables and equations from their crops, and its LaTeX is kept"). After the first commit, the user asked for tables too, as Markdown.

## What was built

- **`pdf transcribe <pdf> --page N --equation 5`**, or `--table 2`, takes the LaTeX or the Markdown the agent read on standard input, as a quoted heredoc gives it:

  ```sh
  pdf transcribe paper.pdf --page 4 --equation 2 <<'EOF'
  \mathcal{L} = -\sum_{i} \log p(y_{i} \mid x_{i})
  EOF
  ```

  - It keeps it by the page's key and the number the page gives the region, in `library.sqlite` in the agent's own folder, beside the picks (`Transcripts`). It holds for the same page in any file; a page that changes leaves it; deleting the cache keeps it.
  - Another transcribe replaces it; `--clear` removes it, even for a region the page no longer shows.
  - The text is kept as written, but for what could reorder, hide or break the output: control and format characters, the characters a renderer ignores, blank braille, and line separators, which become newlines. Tabs become spaces. An equation loses the `$$`, `$`, `\[ \]` or `\( \)` around the whole, unless something inside closes them first, and a table the code fence around it.
  - At most 4,000 Unicode scalars for an equation and 12,000 for a table, so combining marks count; at most 100,000 bytes of input, in UTF-8. Past either, it refuses rather than cuts.
  - It fails, with no usage, when no text comes on standard input or the page holds no such region, naming those it holds. Where a page numbers two regions alike, it keeps the text for the first, which `pdf crop` crops, and says so.
- **`pdf read`** shows it in the region's block, labelled as the agent's reading with its day, the crop still there to check it by. Each line goes out four spaces in, below the block's own `crop:` line and inert in Markdown, so none passes for a page, a block, a command line, a heading or a code fence. LaTeX goes between `$$` lines, so a `%` comment on its last line leaves the display closed, unless it is a display environment of its own, as `align` or `alignat`, or holds its own signs. `--json` gives the block's `latex` or `markdown`, with `transcribed`.

  ```text
  ¶7 [equation 2, transcribed 2026-10-18]
      $$
      \mathcal{L} = -\sum_{i} \log p(y_{i} \mid x_{i})
      $$
    crop: pdf crop paper.pdf --page 4 --equation 2

  ¶12 [table 3, transcribed 2026-10-18] Forecasting results on multivariate benchmarks. Bold: best.
      | Model | ETTh1 | ETTh2 |
      |---|---|---|
      | Ours | 0.412 | 0.338 |
    crop: pdf crop paper.pdf --page 6 --table 3
  ```

- **`pdf crop --equation 5`** and **`--table 2`** print what is kept, to check against the image, or else the `pdf transcribe` command that keeps it; `--json` gives it too.
- **The agent guide** teaches it in two lines, within its 2,000 tokens: 15 sentences elsewhere lost words that carried little. Two details went: that a file slow to open is tried again after 10 minutes, and that exit code 3 also stands for pages each in doubt as a whole. Its test now takes a heredoc, ` <<'EOF'`, as the shell's.
- **Equation numbers with a letter before them,** as "(A.1)" and "(B-1)", are found, which the review found missed since M2.6: parser version 16. On the 38 files of M2.6's sweep, no count changed.
- DESIGNS.md records why standard input, not a flag (a prime, as `f'(x)`, breaks a quoted shell word), why beside the picks, not in the cache (rebuilt at each parser version, and deleted at will), and why four spaces.

**Not in this task:** `pdf search` does not search what is kept. `pdf reparse`, M2.10, should keep transcripts, which read the image, not the text layer.
