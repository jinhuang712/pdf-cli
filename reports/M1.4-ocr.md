# M1.4 — Apple OCR for scanned and garbled pages

- **Landed:** 2026-10-15 in `591a3d4`, with review fixes in `d482349`.
- **Written:** after the fact, on 2026-10-15.

## What was built

- Apple OCR reads a page whose text layer is unusable: a scan with no text, an old OCR layer of stray marks, or a layer of broken characters. Its paragraphs become blocks with boxes mapped back to the unrotated page. The layer it replaced is kept for `--raw`.
- The sample check runs once per new file, in its own worker beside the index. OCR reads a band of one text-rich page, and a file whose layer spells under 70% of OCR's words is read by OCR on every page.
- `testdata/ocr` holds three two-page scans made by `scripts/make-ocr-fixtures.swift` from a CC BY paper.
- Parser version 3, cache schema 3.

## Review

The reviewer confirmed findings 1–3 by running them. It ranked the findings in this order but gave no severities, except *low* for 8.

| # | Finding | Verdict | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OCR images included annotations, but page keys leave annotations out, so a sticky note on one copy of a file showed up in an identical copy's cached text. | Confirmed | Fixed: images for OCR leave annotations out. |
| 2 | When OCR failed, the page failed and its usable text layer was thrown away. Pages larger than about 2,160 pt square always failed, over the 60-megapixel cap. | Confirmed | Fixed: a large page renders at a lower resolution. When OCR fails or reads far less than the layer, the layer serves, marked `pdfkit+ocr-failed`. |
| 3 | On macOS 11–15 the older recognizer had no language set, so Chinese and Arabic scans came back empty and were stored as fine. | Confirmed | Fixed: it detects the language. A test reads a Chinese page. |
| 4 | Contents pages with dot leaders, or forms with blanks, fell under the letters-and-digits threshold and went to OCR. | Likely | Fixed: runs of repeated `.` `_` `-` are left out of the count. |
| 5 | Images inside a form without its own resources, and bitmaps drawn as Type 3 glyphs, were never counted, so such a scan never got OCR. | Possible | Fixed: both count as images. |
| 6 | A sample worker killed for silence left the file counted as trusted, for good. | Possible | Fixed: a sample check that keeps crashing leaves the file read by OCR instead of trusted. |
| 7 | On a turned page, the sample band compared whole OCR paragraphs with the layer's lines. | Possible | Fixed: the band keeps OCR's lines. |
| 8 | The two OCR engines' confidences can't be compared: 0.52–0.68 against 1.00 on the same clean text. | Low | Fixed: each OCR'd page names its engine. M1.3 later dropped confidence as a quality signal. |
