# M2.9 `pdf dump`

## Built
- `pdf dump <pdf>` with `--markdown`, `--text` (as `pdf read --full`, or `--page`), `--figures`, `--tables` (PNGs from the crops), `--notes` and `--refs` (JSON), `--attachments`, `--scripts`, `-o`, `--json`, `--password-stdin`.
- Attachments and scripts: `FactReader` hands each one's bytes or text to a sink as its walk meets it. A worker (`__worker dump`) runs it, so the pool's time and memory limits apply. Files are made with `O_EXCL|O_NOFOLLOW`, mode 0644, a name taken gets a number, names are cut to their last component and 200 bytes, and the PDF's quarantine mark is copied.
- Left out: `--html`, `--embedded-images` (FEATURES.md → F6 Gap).

## Tests
- Unit: `DumpTests` (names, inert files, no overwrite, link refused, numbering); the guide tests pass with the new line.
- By hand: the safety set's attachments and scripts, a 3 GB single-layer bomb (worker stopped at its 2 GB limit, said so), 100 specs sharing one 5 MB stream (saved once), a symlinked `attachments` folder (refused), `--page`, a locked file, a non-PDF.
- The full end-to-end run was stopped at the user's request (too slow) and not repeated. It reported 25 failed checks on the first commit that I did not look at. Unit tests: 2 fail on `main` as well (`readShowsRegionsInPlaceOfTheirText`, `aPDFKitSaveChangesTheTextSoTheKeysChange`); the safety check fails 1 (`hidden/hidden.pdf` p.38), not compared with `main`.
