## What was built

- `pdf reparse <pdf> [--page 3,7-9] [--step ocr] [--password-stdin] [--json]` reads pages again and replaces their cached results. `--step` takes `ocr` only; without `--page` it does every page.
- The command asks a runner of its own (`__runner --reparse`) to read the pages through the shared pool, and waits. The runner keeps every new reading in memory and replaces the results in one transaction at the end (`Store.replaceResults`), with the keyword index following through the table's trigger. A command that is stopped, or killed (the runner sees its parent go), changes nothing, and no runner is left parsing.
- The page keeps its key, so transcripts, kept by key, show again at once. The picks on the pages replaced are cleared after the replacement.
- A file never read is indexed first (an index-only runner), then only the pages asked for are parsed. A failed entry is tried again. The summary line, a list capped at 20 and the JSON are in FEATURES.md → F10; plan/decisions/051-a-reparse-keeps-the-page-s-key-and-replaces-results-in-one.md → Decision: a reparse keeps the page's key and replaces results in one transaction".
