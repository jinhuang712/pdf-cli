# M1.9 — `pdf crop` for pages and regions

- **Built:** 2026-10-15.
- **Written:** 2026-10-15, on the task's branch.

## What was built

- `pdf crop <pdf> --page N`, with `--bbox x,y,width,height` for a region and `--grid` for lines labelled in page fractions. It prints the image's absolute path, its size and its image tokens; `--json` gives the same.
- A worker draws the image (`__worker crop`), in a slot of the shared pool, within the page's time and memory limits.
- Images fit Claude's standard limits: 1,568 px on the long edge and 1,568 tokens of 28×28 px. A region is enlarged up to 6 times.
- A region is in fractions of the page as stored, before its rotation, the frame block positions and failure blocks use; the image comes out upright, with comments and highlights as a reader sees them.
- Images live in `crops/` in the cache folder, one folder per version of a file, for 7 days. The same crop within them answers from the folder, and `pdf gc` removes older ones.
- `--password-stdin` opens a locked PDF, passing the password to the worker in its environment.
- Found while building: a command that asked the reader for no pages, to get only the index, sent the runner `--pages=`, which the argument parser refused, so the read failed after three tries. Fixed in Reader: the empty list is left out.
