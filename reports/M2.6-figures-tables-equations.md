# M2.6 — Figures, tables and equations as blocks with their crops

- **Built:** 2026-10-18, on the task's branch.
- **Written:** 2026-10-18, on the task's branch, after the review's fixes. Under the user's rule of 2026-10-17, tests are light until the final wrap-up: unit and end-to-end tests, one quick review.

## What was built

**The user's choices.** Equations come in three steps, in the user's words: find where each is, recognize it, and turn it into LaTeX, from which the formula can be drawn again; models read LaTeX well, and drawing it back is how the tool checks itself. This task takes the first two: a numbered equation is a block with its number and its crop. The third is the new M2.13. Alt text is hidden text: parsed, labelled as such, and shown only with `--include-hidden`; the user chose this over showing it by default with a label.

**Detection** runs in the parse, from the page's lines and drawing commands, with no model, and is stored with the page's text (`Regions`, parser version 15):

- A caption is a line that starts "Figure 4", "Fig. 4", "Fig 4", "Table 2", "TABLE IV", or the same in Chinese, Japanese, Russian, German, French or Spanish, with the lines below it that go on with it. A line that only wraps an in-text reference under running text is none. Every caption is a block of its own.
- A figure is M1.14's pictures nearest its caption in its column, within 15% of the page, but no line of running text. With no picture there, as a chart whose bars carry their values, it is the shapes the page draws there, joined within 6 pt.
- A table is the horizontal rules that chain from its caption, about as wide as the first, until a gap of 30% of the page, another caption, or two lines of running text across it. A table ruled only around its header goes on down its rows.
- A numbered equation is a line whose "(N)" stands 1.5 em or more apart at its end, or alone, with the lines of maths above and below it and the rest of their rows, in its column.

**`pdf read`** shows each as one block where it starts, in place of the text inside it:

```
¶12 [table 3] Forecasting results on multivariate benchmarks. Bold: best.
  crop: pdf crop paper.pdf --page 6 --table 3
¶7 [equation 2] crop: pdf crop paper.pdf --page 4 --equation 2
```

- The text inside goes aside, counted in the summary line, `set aside: 4 blocks of text inside figures, tables and equations (show: --raw)`, and `--raw` shows it in place, labelled `[table text: rows and columns lost]`, `[figure text]` or `[equation text: its layout lost]`.
- A block that holds hidden text is never set aside: its markers stay in the text.
- Image and drawing markers inside a figure or a table go: the region's crop covers them.
- A read that starts inside a region, as from a search hit, shows the region's block.
- A table's header row, repeated on each page of a long table, is the table's, no longer a running header (an M2.5 limit). Unsure words inside a region go aside with its text and flag no page.

**`pdf crop --figure 4`**, `--table 2` and `--equation 5` crop one by its caption's number, and print its caption. They look in the pages parsed so far, then in the whole file. A number on two pages asks for `--page`; an unknown number fails with the numbers found.

**`pdf info`** lists the figures and tables, at most 40 rows, `--json` all of them. **`pdf search`** names a hit inside one, with its crop command.

**Alt text.** The page's marked content numbers what it draws; `Visibility` records the number of each image and shape drawn at the page's level, and `AltText` maps it through the structure tree's parent tree to its element's `/Alt`, or its nearest parent's. A figure, or a picture outside every figure, with alt text carries a hidden-text marker, counted with the page's hidden places. `testdata/safety/hidden-figure-alt.pdf` plants a line there. The page's cache key holds its alt text (page key 2, index checks 10), so a file that gains alt text, or another file with the same page, is parsed again.

Each rule above was a page an earlier version broke:

- A picture's rect took in its caption's text: regions use M1.14's core rect, the shapes alone.
- An equation took in the other column's line on its row, the display below it and the "where …" after it: column, the next numbered row, a limit below as far as above, and lines starting "where", "with" or "and" as text.
- A bar chart with its values on the bars (2608.22898 p.7) was no picture: the shapes fallback. Its legend counted as running text: only lines across half the figure count.
- A caption under a line ending "self-" read as an in-text reference (2608.24191): a hyphen ends a line too, and the line above must be running text. A PLOS paper's "Fig 1." without a period found no figure (PMC 003): it now finds 11.
- A table on PMC 007 p.13 ran over a paragraph to the rule under the page's footnotes: two lines of running text between end it.
- That stop then lost tables whose rows are set in the body's size: 2608.22898's Table 6 ("Global batch size 512"), 2608.23474's Table 3 (a dataset's name and its numbers) and 2609.23257's Table 22, on a page of nothing else. Running text is now five words or more, most of the line's, and the body's size is read from the lines outside the table and every caption.
- `scripts/smoke-check.sh` caught 2609.23257's p.17 and p.18 no longer flagged unsure: their unsure words are inside tables.

The review's fixes changed no count above but the Russian review's table; on the 38 files, the blocks set aside moved by at most 9 a file, none of them running text.

The cost of detection in the parse is not measured on real files; the review found the smoke files parse in the same time as before M2.6. On synthetic pages after its fixes, debug build: 3,000 lines with 1,500 numbers parse in 2.2 s, and 3,000 lines of plain text in 3.0 s; 1,200 captions add 0.65 s to the 0.67 s of 1,200 plain lines.

## Limits

Recorded in FEATURES.md → F3, F5 and F9:

- A table drawn without horizontal rules is not found; its cells read as text. A table of sentences in the body's size can end at its first such rows, the rest reading as text, the safe side. On the NIST AI framework's p.34, two rows below Table 3's last rule read as text.
- A figure without a numbered caption keeps its pictures' markers; sub-figures are not found one by one.
- A table's notes read as text after its block.
- Pages read by OCR have no regions.
- An equation numbered at its left, whose number PDFKit joins to the next column, or whose number stands closer than 1.5 em to its maths, is not found. A display that PDFKit gives in scraps, as Callaway and Sant'Anna's bracket over three rows (p.10), crops only in part; 2609.22884's equation 7 crops whole but its first symbol.
- Alt text is found only for pictures drawn in marked content at the page's level, and that M1.14 marks as pictures.
- PMC 014 p.13 holds two regions named table A1, a caption and its continuation: `--table A1 --page 13` crops the first.
- A caption's text follows the reading order: where PDFKit reads it out of order ("Attention Is All You Need", p.13–15), it starts mid-sentence.
- `pdf crop --figure N` for a number not found yet parses the whole file first: 115 s for a 517-page textbook. In `TODO.md`.

## Tests

- **Unit:** `RegionsTests`, 9 tests:
  - captions, Chinese, Russian and Japanese ones too, and the in-text references, IEEE labels and "Figure I" that are none;
  - pictures and rules make regions, and one rule makes no table;
  - a table chains past short rows in the body's size and stops at running text in another size (fails on the rule before);
  - a figure takes no paragraph's last line, and no picture beyond a paragraph;
  - a table joins its rules' pieces, and takes neither a rule across the page under it nor a footer's;
  - an equation takes its stacked lines but no running text and no reference, and a header of column numbers is none;
  - the fixture page: a table, a figure with planted alt text, an equation, and the same untagged;
  - `pdf read` shows each with its crop, counts what it sets aside, shows it with `--raw`, counts the alt text as hidden, and a read from inside a table shows the table;
  - a table's header row is no running header.
  - `PageKeyTests`: a page's alt text is part of its key, and the index and the parse agree on it.
  - 210 unit tests in all.
- **Safety set:** `hidden-figure-alt.pdf`, a line planted as a chart's alt text: G16, 72 of 72. `broken-structure-loop.pdf` and `broken-marked-flood.pdf` read in under a second. 26 files.
- **End to end:** `scripts/test-cache.sh` checks, on the fixture and the smoke set, that:
  - a table is one block with its caption and its crop command, its cells set aside, counted, and shown with `--raw`;
  - a numbered equation is a block;
  - `pdf crop --figure` crops with its caption, an unknown number names those found, and a number on two pages asks for `--page`;
  - `pdf info` lists figures and tables, and a search hit in a table names it;
  - a chart whose bars carry their values is a figure (2608.22898 p.7).
